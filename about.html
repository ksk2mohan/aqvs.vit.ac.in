About Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), India

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Vellore is one of India's leading private universities recognized for excellence in education, research, innovation and global engagement. Through its School of Computer Science and Engineering and School of Electrical Engineering, VIT actively promotes interdisciplinary research in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Quantum Computing and Smart Energy Systems.

About Northumbria University, United Kingdom

Northumbria University, Newcastle upon Tyne, United Kingdom, is a research-intensive institution renowned for excellence in engineering, computing and emerging technologies. Through the School of Engineering, Physics and Mathematics, Northumbria contributes expertise in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision and advanced digital technologies while strengthening international collaboration and knowledge exchange.

About the AQVS Project

Advancing Quantum Vision Systems (AQVS): Real-Time Surveillance Skill Building for Smart Energy Facilities is an Indo–UK Transnational Education initiative funded under the British Council Going Global Partnerships programme.

The project brings together Vellore Institute of Technology, Northumbria University and CEIR Mobility Pvt. Ltd. to develop future-ready skills in Quantum Computing, Computer Vision, Artificial Intelligence and Smart Energy Infrastructure Monitoring.

Through technical training programmes, expert lectures, collaborative research, hackathons and digital learning resources, AQVS aims to create a sustainable ecosystem for intelligent surveillance, anomaly detection, predictive analytics and smart energy applications.
