About the Programme

Quantum-inspired learning for next-generation AI systems

The programme introduces participants to quantum-inspired computing, quantum machine learning, quantum algorithms and their practical relevance to artificial intelligence, computer vision, image analysis and smart energy infrastructure. It combines conceptual foundations, mathematical insights, demonstrations, research applications and expert-led discussions.

Participants gain exposure to QML models, hybrid quantum–classical approaches, quantum communication, quantum vision, quantum cryptography, post-quantum cryptography and emerging applications in energy infrastructure.

Programme Highlights

  • 20 expert-led technical sessions across five days
  • Session-wise quizzes and prizes for quiz winners
  • Hands-on demonstrations and application discussions
  • Certificate based on attendance and programme requirements
  • Learning resources, slides, recordings and useful URLs in one place
